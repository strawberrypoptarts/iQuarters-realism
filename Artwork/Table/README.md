# Oak table material

AI-assisted albedo with normal and roughness variants based on that albedo. Original generated images are preserved here; the app uses uniformly resized 1024px copies in Recovery/realism. These are artist-style material maps, not a measured scan or an exact reconstruction of physical height. The old procedural table was rejected after visual review and is not used.

The albedo brief called for an evenly lit, top-down aged oak tavern surface, horizontal planks, varied natural grain and knots, without perspective, props, or directional specular highlights. The normal variant requested restrained tangent-space relief and recessed joints; the roughness variant requested satin varnish with rougher wear and cracks.

No cavity map from the rejected procedural layout is applied. Shadows and screen-space occlusion are computed by SceneKit. Collision geometry remains unchanged.
