# Quarter PBR material

AI-assisted material variants based on the original two-face quarter atlas. The left reverse face remains upside down and the right portrait remains upright to fit the recovered UVs. Original generated files are preserved here; the app uses 2048x1024 packaged maps. The native mesh and collision geometry are unchanged.

Color, tangent-space normal and roughness maps provide clearer face detail, simulated engraving relief and metal wear. These are artist-style approximations, not a measured coin scan or new geometric engraving. Fine inscription alignment and highlights were checked in SceneKit previews; physical-device rendering remains unverified.
